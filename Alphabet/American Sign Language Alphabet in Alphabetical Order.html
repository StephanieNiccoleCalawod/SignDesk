American Sign Language (ASL) Alphabet / ABC Sign Language

Sign languages (or signed languages) are languages that use hand signals to convey meaning. American Sign Language (ASL) is a conmplicated language which uses signs made by moving the hands as well as facial expressions and body postures. American Sign Language is the primary language of deaf North Americans and isused by people who are both deaf and hard-of-hearing. You can view the ABC sign language in the images below.
