1Gesture Matching & Feedback
(Camera Practice Quiz)		1. Log in and start a Camera Practice session
2. The app displays a target letter prompt (e.g., 'Show the letter B')
3. I form the requested ASL handshape in front of the webcam
4. The app compares my detected hand landmarks against the stored reference for that letter
5. When my gesture matches with sufficient confidence, the app confirms 'Correct!' and moves to the next prompt
6. If the match does not meet the confidence threshold, the app shows a visual hint to adjust my hand
7. After all prompts are completed, the session score is displayed		Input:
1. Live webcam feed with detected hand landmarks
2. Stored ASL reference landmark poses for each letter (A–Z)
3. Target letter prompt selected by the quiz engine		Output Display:
1. Target letter prompt displayed prominently on screen
2. Live webcam feed with landmark overlay
3. 'Correct!' confirmation with letter label when gesture is matched
4. Visual hint or adjustment feedback when gesture is not matched
5. Progress indicator (e.g., Letter 4 of 10)
6. Final score summary at end of session		Acceptance Criteria:
1. The app clearly shows me which letter gesture I need to form. (SD-GM-AC1)
2. My hand landmarks are compared in real time against the stored reference for the target letter. (SD-GM-AC2)
3. When my gesture matches at or above the confidence threshold, the app confirms it as correct and advances to the next prompt. (SD-GM-AC3)
4. If my gesture does not match, I see feedback prompting me to adjust my hand position. (SD-GM-AC4)
5. Letter prompts are randomized each session. (SD-GM-AC5)
6. At the end of the session, I can see my total score and accuracy percentage. (SD-GM-AC6)
7. All processing happens locally — no internet connection is required. (SD-GM-AC7)

Error Prompts:
· 'No hand detected. Please position your hand within the camera frame.'
· 'Gesture unclear. Please adjust your hand position and try again.'		SD || User:
