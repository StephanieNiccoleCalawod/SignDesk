2Invalid Gesture Handling
(Camera Practice only)		1. During a Camera Practice session, I perform a hand movement that does not match the target letter
2. The app evaluates my gesture against the stored reference
3. No match is found or confidence is below threshold
4. The app shows a feedback message prompting me to adjust my hand
5. Detection resumes immediately — no gesture is skipped or locked		Input:
1. Webcam hand landmark data
2. Target letter reference pose		Output Display:
1. Feedback message prompting me to adjust my hand
2. No incorrect confirmation — session stays on the current prompt
3. Detection resumes immediately		Acceptance Criteria:
1. Gestures that do not match the target letter do not count as correct. (SD-INV-AC1)
2. I see a visible feedback message when my gesture does not match. (SD-INV-AC2)
3. Low-confidence detections (below 60%) show a warning rather than confirming the gesture. (SD-INV-AC3)
4. The session remains on the current prompt until I form the correct gesture. (SD-INV-AC4)

Error Prompts:
· 'No hand detected. Please position your hand within the camera frame.'
· 'Gesture not matched. Please adjust your hand position and try again.'		SD || User:
