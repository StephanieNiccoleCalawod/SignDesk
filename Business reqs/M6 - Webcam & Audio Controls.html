1Webcam & Audio Controls1. Log in and navigate to Settings

5. If the webcam is disabled, Camera Practice mode becomes unavailable and Flashcard mode remains accessible		User Input:
1. Webcam toggle (on/off)
2. Audio/pronunciation toggle (on/off)		Output Display:
1. Settings interface with labeled toggle switches
2. Active/inactive status indicator for each control
3. Notification when Camera Practice is unavailable due to disabled webcam		Acceptance Criteria:
1. I can enable or disable webcam access from the Settings panel. (SD-SET-AC1)
2. I can enable or disable letter pronunciation audio independently. (SD-SET-AC2)
3. When the webcam is disabled, Camera Practice mode is blocked and a clear message explains why. (SD-SET-AC3)
4. When audio is disabled, the Flashcard and Camera Practice quizzes continue to work normally without sound. (SD-SET-AC4)
5. Settings changes apply immediately without requiring a restart. (SD-SET-AC5)
